Neon Breaker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 July 2026

In short: Neon Breaker collects no personal data whatsoever. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Nothing ever leaves your device.

What data is collected?

No personal data. The app runs entirely on your device. No names, email addresses, location data, contacts or identifiers are collected or transmitted.

Local storage

Your high score, level progress and sound/music settings are stored only locally on your device (browser "localStorage"). This data is never sent to any server and is visible only to you. If you delete the app, it is removed with it.

Network & third parties

  • No connection to external servers while playing.
  • No ad networks and no analytics tools (e.g. no Google Analytics).
  • No third-party SDKs and no social-media integration.
  • All graphics and sound are generated on the device itself.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data — including from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Please reach out through the app's support page on the App Store, or via the project page at github.com/ttravis17/Game.

Neon Breaker is a self-contained arcade game. It requests no permissions, shows no ads, and makes no network requests while you play.
